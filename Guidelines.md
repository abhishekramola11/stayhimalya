# Stay Himalaya — Guidelines
Stance: editorial, dark mode ("night at a hill homestay"). Ink/pine ground, snow-cream text, saffron accent used sparingly for CTAs, flag-red for tiny highlights.
Type: Newsreader (display, italics for emphasis), Hanken Grotesk (body), JetBrains Mono (labels, Wi‑Fi speeds, altitudes).
Layout: asymmetric grids, hairline borders `border-white/10`, generous spacing, photo-led.
